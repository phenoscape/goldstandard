Proposed new terms used in this annotation batch (Skutschas_Gubin_2012.pdf characters).
TEMP IDs are used where no suitable UBERON/PATO term exists after exhausting label, synonym, alternate-spelling, and post-composition searches.

UBERONTEMP:6be80d29-edb2-4da5-ae46-0ceed67a11fb	postatlantal vertebra	Reason: no UBERON term for vertebrae posterior to the atlas as a class. Used with BSPO:0000099 (posterior_to) in post-compositions would require a specific anchor vertebra; as a class for grouping ribs/centra of non-atlas vertebrae, no direct parent captures this. Closest: UBERON:0002412 'vertebra'. Used in char_027, char_043.

UBERONTEMP:3a1a30ab-0e76-41f1-b5f9-46083dfd62e2	four facetted articulation of exoccipital and atlas	Reason: no UBERON or BSPO term for this specific four-facetted articulation. Used in char_058.

UBERONTEMP:aa4c27e3-2202-4667-be4f-0db3ba1af6f7	posterior wall of nasal capsule	Reason: no direct UBERON term for the posterior wall of the nasal capsule as a discrete structure. Closest: UBERON:0002268 'nasal capsule'. Used in char_114.

UBERONTEMP:3acc05fa-cf49-48ac-8c79-893dac26c991	free rib	Reason: no UBERON term for "free rib" as distinct from rib proper. Closest: UBERON:0002228 'rib'. Used in char_134.
