  Label: slot-shaped median fossa
  Ontology: UBERON (anatomy)
  Definition: A slot-shaped median fossa in the braincase into which the endolymphatic ducts open. Found in some early gnathostomes.
  Parent: UBERON:0004704 (bone fossa)
  Used in: char_140.tsv

The quality "slot-shaped" is available as PATOTEMP:37a095ce-53fc-4746-80ff-f7ce2a70ffa4 but the entity itself (the median fossa) needs a new term.
